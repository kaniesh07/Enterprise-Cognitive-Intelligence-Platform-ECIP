# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026)

## Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over to the next year; any remaining balance expires on March 31.
Sick leave is separate: employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Parental leave is 26 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. The company provides a one-time home-office stipend of INR 25,000.

## Expense reimbursement
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days through the Expensify portal. Purchases above INR 50,000 need prior approval from a department head. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## IT security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal. Report suspected phishing to security@acme.example within one hour.
